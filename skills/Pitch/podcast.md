# Podcast pitches

This medium file adds podcast facts and choices when Pitch loads it.

## What is known about the show?

Read the reporter file's base Identity, Role, Record and Routes, and its journalist Outlets, Audience, Formats, Sources quoted, Style, Preferences and Reach. Gather the show's name, host and whether they are a journalist, the addressee's route to booking, listeners, format, contact and booking timing. Read recent episodes and past guests from the file, using only episodes and guests its record actually states, never names in headline lists. A format line saying the desk takes episodes does not establish the show's style. Use the gathering hand-off in Pitch's medium step for other show facts the file lacks; episodes never go on that research list. Name each fact still missing in the package.

For a guest pitch, episode references and the latest-episode test come only from that host's sketch and the episodes confirmed and dated in it. Take the first match for the window and result.

- Media List handed an episode window and latest-episode result, including a confirmation with no limit or `Latest episodes not confirmed within the window`. Keep both unchanged for this run, even when the pitch date differs; do not compute a second result or clear the handed limit.
- No result was handed. Compute it once from the host's file, using the chosen angle package header's coverage window with its inclusive start and end, never newsjacking's separate Window line of hours and a relative deadline. No header window was supplied: use the file's `staleness_days`, otherwise the journalist extension's 30 days. Set the end date to the pitch date and the start date to that end date minus (`staleness_days` minus 1) days, using that chosen number of days. The window includes every day from that start through the end date. State both endpoints and their basis. Does the file confirm the show's latest episodes and date them within that window? Yes: cite the file evidence and record confirmation with no limit. No, including missing or unresolved dates: record `Latest episodes not confirmed within the window`, with the last recorded episode date or its absence.

Keep the window and result in the reader brief and the person's package. Cite only episodes the host's file confirms and dates; the email and subject lines never state the internal mark or limit. Supplied or researched episode lists never supply references or establish the latest episodes, and Pitch's rule that researched show facts prevail over a file's show line never applies to episode lists, this window or this result. The limit alone adds no stop.

## Why do these listeners fit?

Carry the evidence of why the show's listeners match the story's audience to Pitch's fit step, which owns the outcome. Downloads are not listens. Preserve a figure's metric and its date, and its period where stated. A larger show is not better by size.

## What is the episode offer?

Read the requester's material for an episode idea that fits the one decided story and this show's style, the guest and what they bring to that story, and a reference to a past episode or guest the host will recognise. Tie a guest pitch to that story's name and package record in Pitch, never a general offer to appear. An idea the material does not hold is named missing. Source the guest's contribution from that material and the past-episode or guest connection from that host's file; use the record or show evidence for other show facts. A past guest reference must be held as a guest in the file, never inferred from a headline list.

Is each part supported? Take the first match.

- The episode idea, the guest's contribution or the past reference lacks support, or the offer is only a general appearance with no one story. Name each gap. Ask for the material, or a narrower offer or reference the sources hold. Stop until supplied or narrowed. Do not invent a guest, an episode or a connection.
- All three are supported. Carry the offer and its sources into Pitch's angle step. The episode idea describes what a conversation could explore.

## Which approach fits this host?

Ask this gather question once here, unless already answered. Offer an open invitation to discuss the idea or the strongest material first. In that choice, Michael Smart's What non-journalist podcasters think says a non-journalist host may want to develop an idea together, while a journalist wants the strongest material first. Developing it together means an open invitation to discuss, never an offer of questions. Take the first match.

- The file or show evidence establishes a non-journalist host. Recommend the open invitation unless a stated preference points elsewhere. Record the requester's answer; stop until answered.
- The file or show evidence establishes a journalist host. Recommend the strongest material first unless a stated preference points elsewhere. Record the requester's answer; stop until answered.
- Neither is established. Name the host's kind as unknown. Recommend the strongest material first, without assigning a kind to the host. Record the requester's answer; stop until answered.

Keep the evidence, recommendation and answer with the episode offer and ask.

## Is the appearance tied to a launch?

Take the first match.

- It is. Read the material for the launch date, proposed recording and release timing, and any booking window the show states. Name each missing item and ask for a missing launch date; the timetable question is asked only at the lead-time choice. Carry a requested release date to Pitch's What do the terms require?.
- It is not. Keep the show's stated booking timing for the lead-time choice.
- The material does not show whether it is. Ask and stop until answered, then answer this question again.

## Which lead-time choice applies?

Applicable to a first pitch to a podcast. Put the matching choice to the requester at Pitch's contested-choices step. Ask one timetable question covering recording and release, unless already answered. Take the first match.

- The appearance is tied to a launch. Put the booking window beside the requester's proposed timetable. Michael Smart's account of James Clear's launch tour says pitches went out four months before publication and requested a launch-week release. That is one case, not every show's window; top shows may demand to publish first. Recommend working back from the show's stated window. Without one, name it as missing. Record the timetable answer.
- It is not tied to a launch. Put the show's stated booking window beside the requester's proposed timetable. Recommend the sourced window where known. Without one, name it as missing. Record the timetable answer.

## What is added to the package?

Add the show name, host and kind, format, route to booking, contact and timing; the listener and show fit with its evidence; the recent episodes and past guests used with their file citations and episode dates, the episode window with its inclusive endpoints and basis, and the latest-episode result or limit as a note for the person, kept outside the email and subject lines; the story name and angle-package path, session-only record or stated absence of an angle package; the episode idea, guest and their contribution to that story; the past reference; the readiness facts gathered by Pitch and assets; each missing item and any explicit go-on for missing readiness; the launch, recording and release timing and terms; and the host-approach and lead-time answers with their evidence and recommendation. Keep each fact's source, date, hedge and evidence label.
