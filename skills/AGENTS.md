# Skills

Capabilities a user invokes by name for their output; `wiser/standards/primitives.md` owns the type's definition, invocation rules, and frontmatter. The directory is flat, and the index below is grouped by category. Every skill here is owned by an expert whose `Owns:` line names it, or says in its own Context why it stands alone; the Owner column projects that rule. The column is not the declaration.

### Media Relations

| Skill | Description | Owner |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| `Angle Finder/SKILL.md` | A ranked menu of the stories readers on a beat are following and what the sender adds, with coverage, open questions, proof and check results; one checked story angle; a newsjacking package or Newsjacking: no response on one breaking item; or a saved menu updated in re-rank from replies mode. | Stands alone |
| `Campaign Plan/SKILL.md` | One campaign strategy document from a story menu, delivered as a PDF in the sender's bound design, with a worked pitch, claims for sign-off and a short cover-note draft. | Stands alone |
| `Media List/SKILL.md` | A short list of reporters and podcast hosts for one chosen story from the owning root's reporter files, taking story-cited coverage first, with dated work, show fit and episode limits, list-check results, and one Pitch handoff per pick. | Stands alone |
| `Pitch/SKILL.md` | One pitch package from the named journalist or host's sketch for one chosen story, including broadcast and podcast guest pitches, with fit, angle, email and subject lines, follow-ups, terms and desk-check result, plus confirmed-send and reported-reply log rows tied to the story | Stands alone |
| `Reporter Research/SKILL.md` | Write or update one reporter file on one named journalist, a wiser Bio Sketch with this plugin's journalist extension, from their professional public record and the owning root's media database where it has one | Stands alone |
