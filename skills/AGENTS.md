# Skills

Capabilities a user invokes by name for their output; `wiser/standards/primitives.md` owns the type's definition, invocation rules, and frontmatter. The directory is flat, and the index below is grouped by category. Every skill here is owned by an expert whose `Owns:` line names it, or says in its own Context why it stands alone; the Owner column projects that rule. The column is not the declaration.

### Media Relations

| Skill | Description | Owner |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| `Angle Finder/SKILL.md` | One checked angle package from material or a direction, naming what a desk gets, the why-now, the audience, any spokesperson, and the findings to carry into journalist selection and pitching. | Stands alone |
| `Media List/SKILL.md` | A short list of journalists for one angle from the owning root's reporter files, with dated work supporting each pick, the list-check results, and one Pitch handoff per journalist. | Stands alone |
| `Pitch/SKILL.md` | One pitch package, including broadcast and podcast pitches, to one named journalist, producer, host or booker for a story already chosen, with the fit, the angle, the email and subject lines, the follow-ups, the terms, and the desk-check result, plus the pitch-log row once a send is confirmed | Stands alone |
| `Reporter Research/SKILL.md` | Write or update one reporter file on one named journalist, a wiser Bio Sketch with this plugin's journalist extension, from their professional public record and the owning root's media database where it has one | Stands alone |
