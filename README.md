# Media Relations

A WISER plugin for earned media at a company of any kind: pitching reporters, newsjacking, and bylined writing. It loads alongside the `wiser` plugin and uses its writing and research skills.

## What ships

- **Reporter Research** builds on `wiser`'s Bio Sketch to write or update one reporter file on one named journalist, with this plugin's journalist extension: their role, beat, audience, the formats their desk takes, their recent work, stated preferences and contact routes, each fact sourced and dated. It works from the journalist's professional public record and, where your workspace has one, its media database on your own signed-in session. It records nothing about their personal life, marks a reason not to pitch them, such as a retirement or a move out of journalism, and runs `wiser`'s research gate before it writes the reporter file.
- **Pitch** prepares one pitch to one named journalist for a story you have already decided to pitch: why this journalist, the angle, and the email with subject-line options, written by `wiser`'s Content Author in your workspace's voice, or in the voice of the person who pitches for you where your workspace names one. It drafts each follow-up when it is due, offers an exclusive or an embargo only by agreement, and checks every draft against the reporter file before you see it. Where the advice on pitching disagrees, on length, timing or follow-ups, it shows you the positions and a default. It saves each package where your workspace keeps them and, where it keeps none, asks before it writes, offering a `pitches/` folder in your media relations program. It logs a pitch only once you confirm you sent it.

## What it does not do

Nothing in this plugin sends anything. It does not decide whether a story is worth pitching, or to whom, and it does not judge a pitch during a crisis. A press release or an op-ed drafted through Content Author directly gets no newsroom read from this plugin. `system/GAPS.md` lists each of these.

## Install

Point your harness at this repository as a plugin root, or add the repository as a marketplace and install `media-relations@wiser-method-media-relations` from its catalog.

Work lands in the working folder you attach, never in this repository.
